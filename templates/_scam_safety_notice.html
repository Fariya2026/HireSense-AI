CANDIDATE SAFETY

Watch out for fake job postings and recruitment scams

Scammers may impersonate real employers or promise a job after a payment. Never pay registration, training, interview, or application fees, and never share passwords, one-time codes, or banking details with a recruiter.

Verify the vacancy on the employer’s official careers page. Treat pressure to act immediately, personal email addresses, payment links, and WhatsApp-only interviews as warning signs. Trust signals are clues, not a guarantee.
